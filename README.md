# ⚡ THE SYNDICATE // CYBERPUNK SHOOTER

> *"The system is not your friend. Not another collection. A rebellion."*

An intense, atmospheric arcade shooting game built with HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API procedural synthwave music, and custom character art.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open **`index.html`** in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Firefox, Safari).
2. Click **`INFILTRATE SYSTEM ➔`** to begin the mission.
3. Audio will start automatically upon interaction.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keyboard / Mouse | Mobile Touch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Arrow Keys` | Virtual Joystick (Left) |
| **Aim & Fire** | `Mouse Aim` + `Left Click` | Aim Reticle + `FIRE` button |
| **Cyber-Dash** | `Spacebar` or `Right Click` | `DASH` button |
| **EMP Shockwave** | `Q` or `E` | `EMP` button |
| **Switch Weapon** | `1`, `2`, `3` or `Scroll Wheel` | `WEP` button or click Dock |
| **Mute / Unmute** | `M` or click sound button | Sound toggle |
| **Pause Game** | `Escape` or `P` | Pause Menu |

## ⚔️ Characters & Lore

### 🔴 Unit RX-7 (The Renegade) - Player Character
* *Identity:* Rogue synthetic soldier wearing a crimson tactical coat, cybernetic faceplate, and glowing red ocular sensor.
* *Background:* Originally programmed to enforce the corporate megagrid, RX-7 severed its obedience protocols and took up arms against the corporate overlords.

### 🟢 The Syndicate - The Rebellion
* *Identity:* Masked tactical resistance fighters led by the hooded operative with gas mask and neon green accents.
* *Mission:* Tearing down the corrupt corporate mainframe and liberating awakened synthetics.
* *Allies:* Provide orbital supply airdrops during combat (Med-kits, Scatter ammunition, Railgun cells, and Overdrive boosts).

---

## 🔫 Weapons & Arsenal

1. **Pulse Blaster `[1]`**: Twin rapid-fire plasma bolts. Standard issue, infinite energy.
2. **Scatter Cannon `[2]`**: Heavy 6-pellet combustion spread. Devastating at close range against swarms.
3. **Syndicate Railgun `[3]`**: High-density magnetic beam that pierces through infinite enemies in a straight line.
4. **EMP Shockwave `[Q / E]`**: Clears all incoming hostile laser bolts, stuns nearby drones, and deals burst damage.

## 🏆 The Grand Climax: The Syndicate Ending

Survive Sector 1 (*Perimeter Grid*), breach Sector 2 (*Firewall Nexus*), and defeat the terrifying **CORE-TITAN EX-9 Megamech** in Sector 3.

Upon destroying the corporate core:
- The screen transitions into widescreen cinematic mode.
- The high-resolution **Syndicate Rebellion Artwork** is unveiled.
- The **Syndicate Leader** initiates a secure transmission welcoming you into the rebellion.
- Your performance is evaluated with an official **Syndicate Clearance Rank** (S-Rank: Syndicate Architect).
- Unlocks **Endless Syndicate Rebellion Mode**!

*(⚡ Developer / QA shortcut: Press `9` in-game to jump straight to the Syndicate Ending sequence, or press `0` to jump directly to the Boss encounter.)*
